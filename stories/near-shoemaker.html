THE MISSION RECORD

NEAR Shoemaker

Dates
Launched 17 Feb 1996. Landed on Eros 12 Feb 2001. Last contact 28 Feb 2001.
Location
Resting on the surface of asteroid 433 Eros.
What happened
It kept sending data from the surface for about two weeks until the extreme cold ended the mission.
Fun fact
It was the first spacecraft to orbit and land on an asteroid, even though it was never built to land.

What we learned

NEAR studied asteroid Eros up close for a year, mapping its shape and what it is made of. Asteroids like Eros are leftover building blocks from when the planets formed.

What the evidence shows

During its final descent it sent pictures showing objects as small as about 1 cm.

A spacecraft that survived landing

NEAR Shoemaker was built to orbit Eros. It survived its controlled touchdown and continued returning data from the surface for about two weeks.
