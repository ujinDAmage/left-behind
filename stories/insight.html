THE MISSION RECORD

InSight

Dates
Landed 26 Nov 2018. Last contact 15 Dec 2022.
Location
Elysium Planitia · about 4.50238° N, 135.62343° E.
What happened
Dust built up on the solar panels until the lander could no longer generate enough power to communicate.
Fun fact
Its seismometer detected 1,319 marsquakes and helped scientists study the interior of Mars.

Why the lander listened

Seismic waves travel through Mars. Researchers use changes in how the waves travel to infer properties of the planet’s interior.

What the evidence shows

The NASA end-of-mission report records the number of marsquakes and the last contact date.

Mission history book

A station in Elysium Planitia

InSight landed on 26 November 2018. Its robotic arm placed a seismometer directly on the ground.

Listening below the surface

The instrument detected 1,319 marsquakes. Scientists used the way seismic waves travelled to study the crust, mantle and core.

Dust on the panels

As dust accumulated on its solar panels, the lander generated less power. Its final contact with Earth came on 15 December 2022.
