THE MISSION RECORD

Surveyor 3

Dates
Landed 20 Apr 1967. Last contact 4 May 1967. Visited by Apollo 12 on 20 Nov 1969.
Location
Surveyor crater, Ocean of Storms · about 3.01° S, 23.42° W.
What happened
When the lunar night began, the solar-powered lander fell silent. Apollo 12 later landed about 180 metres away.
Fun fact
It is the only robotic probe ever visited by astronauts on another world.

What we learned

Surveyor 3 dug the soil with a scoop and showed the ground was firm enough for astronauts to land on. Apollo 12 brought its camera and scoop home so scientists could study how materials change after years on the Moon.

What the evidence shows

Apollo 12 photos show Pete Conrad standing beside Surveyor 3, with the lunar module in the distance.

A visit two years later

Surveyor 3 sent back 6,326 pictures. Apollo 12 astronauts later removed its camera and soil scoop for study on Earth.
