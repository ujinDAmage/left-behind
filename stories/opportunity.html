THE MISSION RECORD

Opportunity

Dates
Landed 25 Jan 2004 UTC. Last contact 10 Jun 2018.
Location
Perseverance Valley, Endeavour Crater · about 2.331° S, 5.349° W (final site).
What happened
A planet-wide dust storm cut the sunlight available to its solar panels. NASA declared the mission complete on 13 Feb 2019.
Fun fact
Designed for 90 Martian days, Opportunity travelled 45.16 km over more than 14 Earth years.

Why sunlight mattered

Opportunity depended on solar power. The 2018 dust storm reduced the sunlight reaching its panels, and its last contact came while it was at Perseverance Valley.

What the evidence shows

NASA’s traverse map traces its path from Eagle Crater to the final site. The imagined log uses a fictional voice.
