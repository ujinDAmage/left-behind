THE MISSION RECORD

Viking 1 Lander

Dates
Landed 20 Jul 1976. Last transmission 11 Nov 1982.
Location
Chryse Planitia · about 22.5° N, 48.0° W.
What happened
The stationary lander sent images and scientific data for over six years before contact ended.
Fun fact
It made the first successful soft landing on Mars and returned the first image from its surface.

What a lander can learn

A stationary spacecraft can repeatedly observe one location. Viking paired images with science and weather measurements from the same place.

What the evidence shows

The mission record identifies the landing site and final transmission.

Mission history book

A landing at Chryse Planitia

Viking 1 landed on 20 July 1976. Minutes later its camera recorded rocks and one of the lander’s footpads.

One site, years of measurements

The stationary lander took images and used instruments to investigate the soil, weather and conditions at its landing site.

The last transmission

The lander’s final transmission arrived on 11 November 1982, more than six years after touchdown.
