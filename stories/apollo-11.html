THE MISSION RECORD

Apollo 11 Descent Stage

Dates
Landed 20 Jul 1969. Left 21 Jul 1969.
Location
Tranquility Base, Sea of Tranquility · 0.67416° N, 23.47314° E.
What happened
Eagle’s lower half stayed behind when the ascent stage lifted the crew into lunar orbit.
Fun fact
It still carries the plaque commemorating the first crewed Moon landing.

Why it stayed

The lower stage carried the landing gear and descent engine. The upper stage had its own engine for leaving the surface, so the crew could depart without lifting the landing hardware.

What the evidence shows

Look for the legged shape in the orbital record. That shape marks the landing stage.
