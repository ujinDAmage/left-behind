  1. A personal item

    Charlie Duke brought a photo of his family on Apollo 16. He wrote a message on the back identifying them as his family from Earth.

  2. A photo of a photo

    During the mission in April 1972, Duke placed the portrait on the lunar surface and photographed it. The archive records that moment.

  3. The limits of the record

    The portrait stayed on the Moon. The landing coordinates give the area, and the archive photograph shows where it was placed in relation to the ground. Its current condition remains unverified.

THE MISSION RECORD

Apollo 16 Family Photo

Dates
Landed 21 Apr 1972 UTC. Photo left 23 Apr 1972.
Location
Descartes Highlands · about 8.97° S, 15.50° E (landing site).
What happened
Charlie Duke placed a family portrait on the surface and photographed it. Its current condition is unverified.
Fun fact
The back carried a handwritten message identifying it as his family from Earth.

What the photograph records

NASA’s photo of the portrait documents that Duke left it on the lunar surface. Its documented placement and present condition are separate questions.

What the evidence shows

The landing coordinates give the mission area. The archive photo is the exhibit’s direct evidence.
