THE MISSION RECORD

Apollo 17 Lunar Rover

Dates
Landed 11 Dec 1972. Left 14 Dec 1972.
Location
Taurus–Littrow Valley · 20.1896° N, 30.7769° E (final parking spot).
What happened
The crew parked the rover and left it on the Moon when they departed.
Fun fact
Its TV camera recorded the lunar module lifting off.

Why the rover stayed parked

The rover carried people, equipment and a television camera across the valley. At the end, it gave the camera a viewpoint away from the lunar module.

What the evidence shows

The listed coordinates identify the rover’s final parking spot. The interactive trail is an illustration of the journey.
