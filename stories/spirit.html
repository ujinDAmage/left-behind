THE MISSION RECORD

Spirit

Dates
Landed 4 Jan 2004 UTC. Last contact 22 Mar 2010.
Location
Troy, near Home Plate in Gusev Crater · about 14.600° S, 175.525° E (final site).
What happened
Spirit became stuck in soft soil. NASA could not re-establish contact after the rover fell silent.
Fun fact
A wheel dragged through the soil and exposed material rich in silica, a clue to Mars’ watery past.

What the wheel uncovered

Spirit’s dragged wheel exposed silica-rich material. The rover’s observations provide clues about water-related environments on ancient Mars.

What the evidence shows

The final site at Troy is distinct from where Spirit landed. The exhibit trail is illustrative.

Mission history book

Into Gusev Crater

Spirit landed in January 2004 and explored Gusev Crater. A wheel later stopped turning, so the rover dragged it across the surface.

A clue exposed by a wheel

The dragged wheel exposed silica-rich material. Researchers studied it for clues about water-related environments on ancient Mars.

A stop at Troy

In 2009 Spirit became stuck in soft soil near Home Plate. Its last contact was on 22 March 2010. NASA’s recovery attempts received no reply.
