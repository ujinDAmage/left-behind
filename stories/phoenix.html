THE MISSION RECORD

Phoenix Lander

Dates
Landed 25 May 2008. Last signal 2 Nov 2008.
Location
Vastitas Borealis, the Martian arctic · about 68.2° N, 125.7° W.
What happened
As winter came, there was not enough sunlight for its solar panels and it stopped communicating.
Fun fact
It dug up bright chunks that vanished in about four days, showing they were water ice.

What we learned

Phoenix confirmed water ice just below the surface, watched snow fall from Martian clouds, and found perchlorate salts in the soil. These are important clues for the search for life and for future human explorers.

What the evidence shows

Pictures of the “Dodo-Goldilocks” trench show white lumps that disappeared over four days.
