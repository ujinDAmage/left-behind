  1. A passenger to Mars

    Ingenuity rode to Mars under the Perseverance rover and landed in Jezero Crater in February 2021.

  2. Lift-off

    On 19 April 2021 it hovered above the ground, making the first powered, controlled flight on another planet.

  3. Seventy-two flights

    It flew 72 times, about 17 km in total, and scouted the way for the rover. In January 2024 a damaged rotor ended its flying days.

THE MISSION RECORD

Ingenuity Helicopter

Dates
Arrived 18 Feb 2021. First flight 19 Apr 2021. Last flight 18 Jan 2024.
Location
Jezero Crater, Mars.
What happened
On its 72nd flight a rotor blade was damaged, so it can no longer fly. It still stands upright on Mars.
Fun fact
It was planned to fly 5 times. It flew 72 times.

What we learned

Ingenuity proved that powered, controlled flight works in Mars’s very thin air. Future missions can now plan flying scouts to explore places rovers cannot reach.

What the evidence shows

Perseverance photographed Ingenuity many times, including a famous selfie of the two together.

From test flight to scout

The helicopter was designed for up to five flights. It completed 72 and helped scout terrain ahead of Perseverance.
