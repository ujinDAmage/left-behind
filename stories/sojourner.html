  1. Pathfinder lands

    Mars Pathfinder landed at Ares Vallis on 4 July 1997. Sojourner rolled down a ramp the next day and became the first wheeled vehicle to operate on Mars.

  2. Close to the rocks

    The rover moved between nearby rocks and used its instruments to study their composition. Pathfinder relayed commands and results between the rover and Earth.

  3. The relay goes quiet

    The final data from Pathfinder reached Earth on 27 September 1997. Sojourner’s final movements and resting spot remain unconfirmed.

THE MISSION RECORD

Sojourner

Dates
Pathfinder landed 4 Jul 1997. Last data from Pathfinder 27 Sep 1997.
Location
Ares Vallis · about 19.33° N, 33.55° W (Pathfinder landing site).
What happened
The rover relayed data through Pathfinder. When the lander stopped transmitting, Earth lost its link to the rover. Its final position is unconfirmed.
Fun fact
It was the first wheeled vehicle on Mars and operated for 83 days on a planned seven-day mission.

Why the rover went quiet

The lander served as the rover’s link to Earth. The last Pathfinder transmission sets the limit on what the mission could report about the little rover’s later movements.

What the evidence shows

The coordinates are for Pathfinder. Treat Sojourner’s final location as unconfirmed.

A bouncy landing

Pathfinder bounced on airbags before stopping in Ares Vallis. Sojourner’s name honours abolitionist and civil rights advocate Sojourner Truth.
