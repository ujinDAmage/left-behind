THE MISSION RECORD

Apollo 15 Laser Reflector

Dates
Landed 30 Jul 1971. Deployed 31 Jul 1971.
Location
Hadley Rille · 26.1334° N, 3.6285° E.
What happened
The crew set up the passive reflector so Earth observatories could bounce laser pulses off it and measure the Moon’s distance.
Fun fact
Its 300 corner cubes send light back toward its source without a power supply.

How ranging works

A corner cube reflects light back toward the incoming direction. Observatories time the round trip and divide by two to recover the one-way distance.

What the evidence shows

The demo uses simulated distances and the vacuum speed of light. It illustrates the principle behind lunar laser ranging.
