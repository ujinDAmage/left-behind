THE MISSION RECORD

Apollo 14 Golf Balls

Dates
Landed 5 Feb 1971. Hit and left 6 Feb 1971.
Location
Fra Mauro · about 3.65° S, 17.47° W (landing site).
What happened
Alan Shepard hit two golf balls during the final moonwalk and left them on the surface.
Fun fact
He used a six-iron head attached to a sampling-tool handle and swung with one hand.

Why the swing looked awkward

A pressurised spacesuit restricts movement. Shepard improvised a club and swung one-handed in bulky gear.

What the evidence shows

The listed coordinates mark the landing area. Use that distinction if you put the balls on a map.
