Left Behind is a student exhibition for the Sarawak Space Challenge 2026.

Mission evidence

Each exhibit links to the mission records, reference maps and coordinate sources that support its research. For small objects with unverified coordinates, the location is labelled as a reference site.

Story and simulation

Character voices and dialogue are fictional. The crescent trails are illustrative. The laser activity uses simulated distances and the vacuum speed of light.

AI disclosure

ChatGPT assisted with code, interface design and fictional story drafts. Planet graphics and souvenir frames are rendered in code. Photo credits appear with the corresponding exhibit as pictures are added.
