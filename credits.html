Left Behind is a student exhibition for the Sarawak Space Challenge 2026.

Mission evidence

Each exhibit links to the mission records, reference maps and coordinate sources that support its research. For small objects with unverified coordinates, the location is labelled as a reference site.

Story and simulation

The books retell documented mission history. Paper models and equipment activities simplify the actual mechanisms. The laser activity uses simulated distances and the vacuum speed of light.

AI disclosure

ChatGPT assisted with code, interface design and website and story drafts. Paper scenes, planet graphics and souvenir frames are rendered in code. Each exhibit credits and links to its archive image.
